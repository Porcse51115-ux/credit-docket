Frequently Enforced Questions

By federal statute (15 U.S.C. § 1681i), consumer reporting agencies must complete their reinvestigation within 30 days of receiving your notice. Credit Docket automatically begins this countdown the exact second USPS confirms delivery of your certified mail dossier. If no verified proof of investigation is returned within the deadline, our engine automatically prepares statutory deletion demands and CFPB administrative complaints.
We intentionally use physical USPS Certified Mail with barcode tracking. Bureau e-Oscar web portals force consumers to categorize complex inaccuracies into oversimplified 2-digit codes, waiving arbitration and statutory audit rights. Physical dossiers, printed and mailed directly to bureau legal departments, mandate human verification under Metro 2 guidelines and preserve a rock-solid chain of evidence.

Sovereign Pro and Enterprise members have automated Method of Verification (MOV) rebuttals triggered under 15 U.S.C. § 1681i(a)(3). Our system compiles this failure into an evidentiary docket ready for consumer attorney review.
