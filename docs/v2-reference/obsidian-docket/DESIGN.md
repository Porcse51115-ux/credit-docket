## Brand & Style

This design system blends high-luxury institutional private banking aesthetics with modern, cinematic fintech design. It is engineered for credit rebuilding, legal disputes, and credit health orchestration. The visual environment evokes power, precision, uncompromised agency, and technological authority.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Discerning consumers, legal advocates, and financial climbers actively reclaiming their credit velocity and navigating FCRA dispute workflows.
- **Emotional Response:** Sovereign financial control, unyielding confidence, crystalline clarity, and an elevated sense of VIP prestige. 

### Design Philosophy: Cinematic Glassmorphism & Sovereign Precision
The visual language fuses deep atmospheric space with crisp geometric instrumentation:
- **Cinematic Dark Immersion:** Pure obsidian and cosmic navy backdrops set an expansive, distraction-free stage.
- **Frosted Translucent Armor:** Glassmorphic surfaces with razor-thin, light-reactive borders establish physical layering and structural pedigree.
- **Sovereign Accentuation:** Emerald and mint luminescence designate positive legal resolution and trajectory acceleration, paired with electric gold/amber indicators for live dispute friction and institutional leverage.

## Colors

The palette leverages an obsidian deep-space canvas punctuated by razor-sharp functional radiance.

### Canvas & Surfaces
- **Obsidian Canvas (`#0a0d14`):** The absolute foundational background, grounding all screens in deep cinematic darkness.
- **Midnight Navy Surface (`#0f172a`):** The primary container base, creating deep structural separation from the raw canvas.
- **Translucent Plate Tint (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.65)`): Applied to glassmorphism cards with backdrop blur.
- **Glass Edge Stroke (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`): Ultra-crisp hairline container perimeter that reflects ambient interface glow.

### Accents & Indicators
- **Emerald Mint (`#10b981` / `#34d399`):** Primary action and triumph indicator. Designates upward credit score momentum, successful bureau deletions, validated items, and authoritative calls to action.
- **Electric Amber (`#f59e0b`):** Secondary tension indicator. Highlights active statutory dispute timelines, 30-day FCRA reinvestigation countdowns, warning signals, and financial leverage items.
- **Text Hierarchy:**
  - **Text Primary (`#f8fafc`):** 96% white with cold blue undertones for titles, critical scores, and metric integers.
  - **Text Secondary (`#94a3b8`):** Muted slate for metadata, tracking subheads, and secondary metric labels.
  - **Text Tertiary (`#475569`):** Dark slate for legal disclaimers, timestamps, and de-emphasized bureau notes.

## Typography

The typography pairs the regal authority of a contemporary high-contrast serif with the surgical, analytical cadence of a technical geometric sans-serif.

### Pairing Logic
- **Headlines & Mastheads (`Playfair Display`):** Carries the legal pedigree and historical prestige of court dockets and private banks. Applied to core platform mastheads, section headers, and celebratory milestone titles.
- **Metrics, Data, & Body (`Space Grotesk`):** Provides monospaced-adjacent tabular precision for bureau readings, credit scores, dispute timers, and navigational pills. Ensures dense financial tracking reads with absolute legibility.

### Tracking and Micro-Copy
Labels such as bureau tags, dispute status pills, and card eyebrow categories utilize `label-lg` or `label-sm` with all-caps uppercase styling and deliberate wide letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.1em`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model establishes a unified, balanced 12-column analytical dashboard with wide canvas breathing room.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid capped at `1440px` max-width. Standard gutters sit at `1.25rem` (`20px`) with outer canvas margin at `2rem` (`32px`). Multi-metric cards utilize 3-column spans (4 items per row) or 6-column spans for side-by-side dispute monitors.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout. Metric cards collapse into 4-column spans (2 items per row).
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column reflow with full-width stacked cards. Side canvas margins contract to `1rem` (`16px`), while gutter spacing sits at `0.75rem` (`12px`).

### Vertical Rhythm
Section blocks are spaced by `space-xl` (`2.5rem`). Component internals leverage strict multiples of 4px, anchoring metric values cleanly beneath uppercase label chips.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses frosted glassmorphic suspension rather than heavy drop shadows, mimicking precision lenses layered atop dark celestial surfaces.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Backdrop):** `#0a0d14` with subtle radial lighting gradients (e.g., top-center ambient emerald blur at 4% opacity).
- **Tier 1 (Base Plates & Metric Cards):** `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.65)` frosted glass with `16px` backdrop-filter blur and a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Tier 2 (Elevated Focus / Interactive Hover):** `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.75)` with `20px` backdrop blur, subtle top-edge inner highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`), and a soft ambient shadow (`0 12px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`).
- **Tier 3 (Active Overlays & Modals):** `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.92)` with `24px` backdrop blur, framed with an active accent glow (e.g., `0 0 40px -10px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)` for positive flows or amber for active dispute dockets).

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, modern geometric rounding to humanize the interface while preserving structural authority.

### Radii Application
- **Input Fields, Small Badges, & Chips:** `0.5rem` (8px) for compact, calibrated tactile surfaces.
- **Metric Tiles, Containers, & Primary Glass Cards (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (16px) creating clean, tailored visual envelopes.
- **Modal Dialogue Frames & Outer Workbench Modules (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` (24px) for expansive layout framing.
- **Navigation Controls & Action Buttons:** Sculpted pill-shaped profiles or refined rounded rectangles with micro-chamfered appearances.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (Mint Sovereign):** Background in high-luminance emerald `#10b981` transitioning subtly into `#059669`. Bold, dark obsidian text (`#0a0d14`). Subtle glow on hover (`0 0 20px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary Action (Ghost Frosted):** Background of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` with `1px` border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`. Text in `#f8fafc`. Hover triggers `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Dispute Trigger (Amber Warning):** Semi-transparent amber base (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`) with bright amber border (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.4)`) and amber text (`#fbbf24`).

### Metric & Glass Cards
- Built with frosted glass plates (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.65)` + `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).
- Metric headers carry `label-sm` uppercase text in muted slate (`#94a3b8`).
- Metric counters (0, 780, etc.) render in large `metric-xl` or `metric-lg` using emerald (`#10b981`) for positive resolutions/scores, amber (`#f59e0b`) for items in dispute, and clean off-white (`#f8fafc`) for baseline metrics.

### Segmented Navigation Bar & Pills
- Persistent top-bar docket switchers enclosed inside a single pill frame (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.8)` with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` outline).
- Active item receives an opaque dark fill (`#1e293b`), crisp white text, and an illuminated 2px mint or amber bottom notch indicator.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Recessed slate background (`rgba(10, 13, 20, 0.7)`), 1px stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- Focus state activates an emerald outer border with a smooth `0 0 0 3px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)` focus ring.
- Placeholder text styled with muted technical mono-sans in `#475569`.

### Bureau Tracking Matrices & Status Badges
- **Equifax / Experian / TransUnion Columns:** Nested translucent plates with distinct bureau headers and color-keyed dispute health indicators.
- **Status Badges:** Compact pill chips featuring dot-glow indicators (e.g., a pulsating 6px `#10b981` sphere for "DELETED / RESOLVED" and an amber static dot for "IN 30-DAY FCRA REVIEW").
